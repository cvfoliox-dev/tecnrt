# Human-Centered Academic Copywriting Skill

This skill guides content editing and writing for academic institutions, specifically engineering colleges. It eliminates generic AI-generated buzzwords and corporate slogans, ensuring the copy sounds like a credible, authentic educational institution.

---

## 1. Core Writing Principles

1. **Specific Evidence Over Vague Superlatives**:
   - Never use "beacon of innovation", "fostering excellence", "shaping knowledge, building futures", "excellence in pedagogy", or "world-class cutting-edge".
   - State concrete facts: programs offered, laboratory facilities, faculty credentials, actual placement offers, tuition and scholarships, and student support.
   - Test: *Could this sentence describe any random entity?* If yes, replace it with verified college specifics.

2. **Plain English & Active Voice**:
   - Write short, clear sentences. Use familiar words ("use" instead of "utilize", "help" instead of "facilitate", "need" instead of "require").
   - Prefer active voice: "Students build and test prototypes" rather than "Prototypes are built and tested by students".
   - Address the reader directly where appropriate: "You can apply for B.Tech courses..." rather than distant bureaucratic passive voice.

3. **Restrained, Meaningful Headings**:
   - Headings must immediately inform the reader what the section contains:
     - Instead of "INSTITUTIONAL LEADERSHIP & GUIDANCE" -> "Principal's Message"
     - Instead of "CAREER TRAJECTORY & RECRUITING MILESTONES" -> "Placements"
     - Instead of "CAMPUS BULLETINS & ANNOUNCEMENTS" -> "News and Announcements"
     - Instead of "ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE & PEDAGOGY" -> "Teaching and Learning"
   - Avoid repetitive ALL-CAPS kicker eyebrows above every single title unless providing essential category taxonomy (e.g. "Admissions 2026").

4. **Audience-Aware Clarity**:
   - **Prospective Students**: Degree branches, eligibility, application steps, lab practicals, placements.
   - **Parents**: Accreditation, fee structure, scholarships (e.g., ₹60L annual corpus), campus discipline, safety & anti-ragging helpline.
   - **Current Students**: Examination notifications, timetables, portal access, library resources, fee schedules.
   - **Recruiters**: Branch-wise student counts, campus placement records, training highlights, contact details of the Training & Placement officer.

5. **Factual Integrity**:
   - Never invent or fabricate awards, NIRF ranks, placement numbers, or accreditations.
   - Preserve all verified college names, official registration codes (EAPCET Code: TMLN), phone numbers, and historical notices.
